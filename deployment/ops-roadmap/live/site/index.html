HOW TO READ THIS ROADMAP

Source dates. Clear context.

Dates reflect the One Page fields; a date alone does not mean a gateway has passed. Text statuses and N/A are preserved. ETA, month and quarter targets remain estimates.

Budget check marks and Done mean Confirmed. The source blue budget status means Partial. CAPEX, OPEX and Vendor are shown separately from confirmation status. Gateway Partial statuses are shown as WIP. An empty cell means Not provided.

Month/day dates without a year use 2026; source inconsistencies are flagged.
