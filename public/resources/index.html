Roster coverage & calculation rules

This is a deduplicated list of named resources observed in the four published Q4 planning domains. It is not a certified total headcount for all Charley domains; domains and people not yet supplied cannot be counted. Unknown role / category / effort remains visible.

Only confirmed allocations enter the reported Q4 total. Corporate and SCM Ops owners can be listed before their comparable Q4 plan and Dedicated / Shared classification are confirmed. SCM Ops follows its displayed role scope (Ops Leader, PM, BA, TL, QA, L1.5 and Finance); the previously excluded Dev scope stays excluded.

Known aliases are merged: Bi Yu / Eunice Bi, Wang Xian / Xian Wang, Yang Ping / Ping Yang and Daisy / Dasiy / Daisy Tang. Unresolved short names such as Yola, Liv and Kenny are retained without inventing a full identity. Team / vendor placeholders do not enter named-resource headcount.

Shared total = sum of confirmed Shared / Shared QA MD from each domain. Dedicated effort is not added to Shared totals. Mixed classifications are flagged for review. Multiple roles in a domain do not duplicate a person’s domain-level personnel plan.
